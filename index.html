About McCauley Street

An operator's judgment, available fractionally.

Rushton McGarr is a C-level executive and board member who has spent two decades scaling private equity and venture-backed businesses. He translates a strategic objective into the operating architecture required to reach it, and builds the team accountable for getting there, drawing on experience as a CFO, President, and COO across quantum technology, enterprise SaaS, and energy.

He is currently the Chief Financial Officer of Ascend Analytics, backed by Rubicon Technology Partners, and previously led finance at Vitruvi Software (Bow River Capital), Infleqtion (NYSE: INFQ), and FullContact, both of which are Foundry Group portfolio companies. Across those roles he has closed equity and debt financings, run audits and installed governance, integrated acquisitions, implemented EOS, and rebuilt management-to-board reporting and credibility.

Investors engage him when a portfolio company needs senior judgment quickly. He now offers that experience on a fractional basis: embedded operating and financial leadership for portfolio companies, funds, and their boards, scaled to what the business actually requires.

  • 20+ years as a CFO, President & COO
  • PE-owned and VC-backed operator and board member experience
  • Industries: quantum, enterprise SaaS, energy analytics, identity & data
